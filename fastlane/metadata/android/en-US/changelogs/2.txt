First public release (1.0.1).
- Offline TOTP codes, three at a time (previous, current, next).
- Add accounts by QR code or by hand. Show a secret or QR code again to move it to another device.
- Encrypted backup (format v2 with tamper detection). 1.0.0 backups can still be read.
- Clock sync by SNTP, only when you press the button. No ads, no tracking.
- New About screen in Settings.
